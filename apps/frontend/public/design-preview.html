N° 04

Honest answers

Yes, the wifi works.Here is the proof.

How we test it

Speed tests, morning and evening

Before any room or homestay joins the programme we test it at both ends of the day, then keep testing while you are there. If it drops, you hear it from us first.

Speed test — to shoot on site

When it fails

A backup on a second network

Every stay has a second connection on a different mobile network, plus power backup that carries a full call. In the village, your biggest calls can move to a town day.

Backup router — to photograph

Power cuts

They happen. We plan for them.

Nepal still has cuts. We would rather tell you that now than have you discover it ten minutes before a client call.

The visa

150 days a calendar year

Nepal's tourist visa allows up to 150 days per calendar year. That is why stays are one, three or five months — and why the longest is arranged across New Year.
