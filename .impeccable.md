# Impeccable — Mountain Whisper

## Design Context

### Users
Comfortable mid-range remote workers, roughly 26–40, from the US, UK, Europe and
Australia: developers, designers, marketers, writers. They earn enough that
~$1,490/month is easy but they still want value. They have heard of Bali and
Chiang Mai and have never seriously considered Nepal, because it looks like
hard work to arrange. They arrive sceptical and leave either reassured or gone.
Six worries decide it: the internet, power cuts, the visa, safety, whether they
can trek without losing a week, and loneliness.

### Brand Personality
Calm, warm, honest — "the way the name sounds". The voice is a friendly local
who has thought of everything: short, plain sentences. "Yes, the wifi works.
Here's the speed test." Never "INSANE DEAL!!!". Honesty is the differentiator:
the brand admits power cuts rather than hiding them.

### Aesthetic Direction
Fixed by `brand/BRAND-COLOURS.md`, which is binding and overrides generic design
preference, including this skill's font guidance:

- Misty blue `#3E5C76` (primary) · Cream `#F5F1EA` · Deep ink `#1A2733` ·
  Mist grey-blue `#DCE5EC` (the italic second voice) · Stone grey `#6B7785`.
  **No other colours** — no red/green/yellow, even for ticks and crosses.
- **Playfair Display** headlines with an italic second line; **Montserrat**
  uppercase letter-spaced labels. Do not substitute: the films use these, and
  the site must read as the same brand.
- Footage: Muted Editorial grade, light grain, soft vignette, and a brand
  blue/ink wash over every shot, darker where type sits.
- Dark, not light. The palette is built for ink; mist grey-blue is unreadable
  on cream.

Anti-reference: the generic travel-site hero — a full-bleed photograph with a
big serif headline on a dark scrim. It is the default template for every
hospitality site and says nothing about this business.

### Hard constraint: image quality
The only high-resolution asset in the project is one 3840×2160 photograph.
Everything else is a frame of compressed 1080×1920 stock video. Large imagery
therefore reads soft. **Design around this**: use imagery small, where heavy
downscaling makes it crisp, and let type and structure carry the page.

### Design Principles
1. Honesty is the aesthetic. If something is not yet true, mark it, don't stage it.
2. The films are the brand. Chapters, kickers, hairline rules, two-voice
   headlines and time beats belong on the page too.
3. Type carries the page; photographs support it. Forced by the asset ceiling,
   and better for the brand anyway.
4. One emphasis per screen (brand rule 1): an italic second line, or one key
   word, or one number. Never all three.
5. Every claim on the page is one the business can keep.
